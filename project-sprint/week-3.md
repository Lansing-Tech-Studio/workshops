# Project Sprint - Week 3

**Duration:** 2 hours\
**Goal:** _"Finalize your Demo Day materials and get ready to present."_

## Tonight's Focus

This is your last work session before Student Demo Day. Use the time to polish everything and make sure you're prepared:

- Finish your Demo Day slides (with your logo and demo video)
- Practice your 5-minute presentation
- Review the Demo Day checklist below

## Step 1: Finish Your Demo Day Slides

- Here is the template on [Google Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Azc8tjCDR7KWaVOq61qzlDant5tqGEIN44F9onDbIWU/edit?usp=sharing)
- Make sure your logo and demo video are included
- Share your final presentation with [brendon@lansingtechstudio.org](mailto:brendon@lansingtechstudio.org)

## Step 2: Share Your Demo Reel

Upload or share your demo recording so it's accessible on Demo Day — don't rely only on having it saved locally.

- Share your demo reel with [brendon@lansingtechstudio.org](mailto:brendon@lansingtechstudio.org)
- Double-check that the video plays correctly in your slides

## Step 3: Practice Your Presentation

You'll have **5 minutes to present** on the big screen, followed by **2 minutes of audience questions**.

- Walk through your slides out loud — say everything you plan to say
- Practice answering questions like:
  - "What problem does your project solve?"
  - "What was the hardest part to build?"
  - "What would you add if you had more time?"
- Aim for a natural, confident delivery — it doesn't have to be perfect

## Demo Day Checklist

**Student Demo Day** is at **14 Crescent Rd, East Lansing, MI 48823**

### Schedule

- **4:00 – 4:45 pm** — TechSmith HQ tour
- **4:45 – 5:00 pm** — Questions, break, setup
- **5:00 – 5:30 pm** — Food (sponsored by RMC) and show off projects at tables
- **5:30 – 7:30 pm** — 5-minute presentations on the big screen in the atrium + 2 minutes of audience questions

### What to Bring

- [ ] Your computer (make sure it's charged — bring your charger!)
- [ ] Your presentation and demo reel available on your computer
- [ ] Your Google Slides presentation shared with [brendon@lansingtechstudio.org](mailto:brendon@lansingtechstudio.org)
- [ ] Your demo reel shared with [brendon@lansingtechstudio.org](mailto:brendon@lansingtechstudio.org)
- [ ] Your demo reel should ideally have a shared link embedded in your slides

### What to Wear

Business casual — think polo with dark jeans/khakis, or wear something that fits the theme of your project (e.g. a Minecraft shirt if your project is Minecraft-related).

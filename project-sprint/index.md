# Project Sprint - Student Demo Day Prep

**Duration:** 3 weeks
**Goal:** _"Polish your project, record it in action, and build your demo day presentation."_

## What You'll Do

Over the next 3 weeks, you'll get your project ready to show off at Student Demo Day. This is your time to:

- Add finishing touches and fix bugs in your application
- Record your application in action
- Build a slideshow presentation to walk through what you created

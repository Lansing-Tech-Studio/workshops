# Project Sprint - Week 2

**Duration:** 2 hours\
**Goal:** _"Lock in your Demo Day Project Details and start building your demo materials."_

## Tonight's Focus

This week you'll nail down your Demo Day Project Details and start/continue creating the materials you'll use on Demo Day:

- Submit your project details using the form below
- Design your project logo
- Record/refine a short demo of your project in action
- Work on your Demo Day slides (if you haven't already)

## Step 1: Submit Your Project Details

Fill out this form to lock in your Demo Day Project Details. This tells us what you built, who it's for, and what makes it special.

- [Demo Day Project Submission](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSf57e9Gf2LkdytZMPOQc28SV5ZCdwrDs1djgK1iVj_SuEWnQQ/viewform?usp=sharing&ouid=117503804469181749675)

## Step 2: Design/Refine/Finish Your Logo

Your logo will appear on your Demo Day slide. Keep it simple — a clean icon or wordmark works great.

- Open a drawing app, Google Slides, or any tool you're comfortable with
- Aim for something that represents your project at a glance
- Save it as an image file so you can drop it into your presentation later

## Step 3: Record/Refine Your Demo

A short screen recording/demo (1-2 minutes) showing your project in action is one of the most powerful things you can bring to Demo Day.

- Show the most interesting or useful part of your project
- Narrate what you're doing as you record — just talk through it naturally
- Don't worry about making it perfect; authentic is better than polished

## Step 4: Work on Your Demo Day Slides

- Here is the template on [Google Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Azc8tjCDR7KWaVOq61qzlDant5tqGEIN44F9onDbIWU/edit?usp=sharing)
- Customize the template with your project details, logo, and demo video

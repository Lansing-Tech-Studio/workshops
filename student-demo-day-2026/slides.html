Tonight is a celebration of growth.

Tonight's Format

5 Minutes

Each student shares their project vision, demo reel, and key decisions and learnings.

2 Minutes

Audience questions, letting students reflect, explain, and level up their ideas.

Short transitions between presenters to keep momentum.

Audience Question Prompts

  • What was the best part of your journey?
  • What was your biggest obstacle?
  • Who did you create this project for?
  • What would version 2.0 look like?

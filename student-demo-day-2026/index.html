ASK THE STUDENTS

Spark conversation. Inspire innovation.

Use these questions to dive deeper, challenge ideas, and celebrate our young innovators!

START WITH PURPOSE

Dig Into Their Purpose

  • Why did you choose your project idea to create?
  • During the “Draw It First” phase, was this the original idea you had, or did it evolve into something different?
  • If you changed your idea, why did it change?
  • What is your favorite part of your project that you created?
  • How does your project make life more fun or easier for the person using it?
  • What problem were you most excited to solve, and why?
  • What was a moment when your idea didn’t work—and what did you learn from that?
  • If you had to start over, what would you do differently and why?
  • What trade-offs did you have to make in your design?

DESIGN FOR REAL PEOPLE

Understand Their Impact

  • Is there a specific moment or feeling you want people to walk away with after they try your demo?
  • What kind of feedback challenged your thinking the most?
  • What market research did you do to help put together your project?
  • How did you differentiate your project from other existing games or apps?
  • Who do you think would benefit most from your project and why?
  • What surprised you most while talking to others about your idea?

SOLVE, TEST, MAKE BETTER

Explore Their Process

  • What was the biggest “bug” you ran into during your process, and how did you fix it?
  • If you had one more week to “refine and debug,” what is the first thing you would change about the technical side of this project?
  • How did you test or track progress?
  • If this project went viral tomorrow, what’s one feature you’d add to make it even better for a huge audience?
  • What part of the process are you most proud of and why?

THINK AHEAD, DREAM BIG

Imagine What’s Next

  • What would you change if you had one more week?
  • What would version 2.0 of your project look like?
  • If you had to turn this into a business, what would you sell?
  • Where could this idea go in the next year?
  • What’s one thing you want to learn next to level up?

Your questions can spark big ideas
and brighter futures!

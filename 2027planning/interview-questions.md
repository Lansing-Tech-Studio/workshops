# 2027 Interview Questions and Missing Details

Questions to answer before and during the series. Checkboxes are for tracking
what has been answered; write answers under each item.

- **Part 1** applies to every partner interview.
- **Part 2** lists the gaps specific to each project, split into what to ask
  the partner before the series and what a student fills in after choosing
  the project.
- **Part 3** is for us: details about the series itself.

Project numbers match [project-classification.md](project-classification.md).

## Part 1: Questions for every partner

### People

- [ ] Who is our main contact, and what is the best way to reach them?
- [ ] Is there a time of day or week that works best for them?
- [ ] How quickly can they usually reply to a student question?
- [ ] Are they comfortable communicating with students if a mentor is copied
      on every message?
- [ ] Who else needs to approve the design and/or final result?

### The problem

- [ ] What is happening today, and what is painful about it?
- [ ] Who will use what we make (staff, volunteers, the public, kids)?
- [ ] How many people, and how often?
- [ ] What have they already tried?
- [ ] If we could only deliver one thing, what should it be?

### Done

- [ ] What would make them say "this was worth it" at Demo Day?
- [ ] Is there a date that matters to them (an event, a season, a deadline)?
- [ ] Is a prototype or proof of concept useful, or only a finished product?

### What exists

- [ ] What tools, websites, and accounts do they use today?
- [ ] Can a student be given access, and at what level?
- [ ] Do they have a logo, colors, photos, or existing documents we can use?
- [ ] Is there a budget for hosting, software, or materials?

### Afterward

- [ ] Who maintains it after Demo Day, and how technical are they?
- [ ] What tools would that person be comfortable with?
- [ ] Do they want us to stay involved, and for how long?

### Data and safety

- [ ] Does any of this involve personal information (names, addresses,
      health, housing, payments, children)?
- [ ] Are there rules they must follow about that information?
- [ ] Is there anything students should not see or publish?

### Involvement

- [ ] Can they introduce the problem in week 1 (in person or a 3-minute
      recording)?
- [ ] Can they give feedback in week 8?
- [ ] Can they attend Demo Day?
- [ ] If this project is not picked this series, can it wait?

## Part 3: Series-level questions for us

### Schedule

- [ ] Start date, day of the week, and time.
- [ ] Any skipped weeks (2026 skipped two)?
- [ ] Is Demo Day session 12 or an additional session?
- [ ] Is there a parent prep night, and when?
- [ ] Will there be optional in-person office hours (retro request)?

### Students

- [ ] Expected cohort size and age range.
- [ ] How many are returning from 2026, and what did they build?
- [ ] How do we learn interests and skill levels before week 1?
- [ ] How are projects assigned when several students want the same one?
- [ ] What does a student do if their project stalls or their partner goes
      quiet?

### Mentors and volunteers

- [ ] Names and confirmed availability for the advertising volunteer and the
      UX / human-centered AI volunteer.
- [ ] Which weeks can Adam and Joe Dearman lead?
- [ ] How many projects can run at once given the number of mentors?
- [ ] Does every group project get a named mentor?
- [ ] Background check and youth protection requirements for new volunteers.

### Projects

- [ ] Which projects are must-do this series, and which can wait?
- [ ] What happens to projects nobody picks?
- [ ] Do we agree in writing with partners on scope and on what happens
      after Demo Day?
- [ ] Who owns what students create, and where is it hosted?

### Tools and access

- [ ] Which accounts do students need, and which have age restrictions
      (retro dislike)?
- [ ] What laptops will students bring, and do the chosen tools run on all
      of them (retro dislike)?
- [ ] Which AI tools, and how do we handle token limits (retro dislike)?
- [ ] Which tools for forms, spreadsheets, and video editing?
- [ ] Rules for students contacting partners and handling partner data.

### Tracking and feedback

- [ ] How do we record progress each week (journal, photos, check-in form)?
- [ ] When do we collect student, parent, and partner feedback?
- [ ] Will there be a retrospective like 2026's?

Project-specific gaps. Also ask the common questions in
[interview-questions.md](../interview-questions.md). Project numbers match
[project-classification.md](../project-classification.md).

      Word agreement and invoice templates?

- [ ] Which step takes the most time or goes wrong most often?
- [ ] How do sponsors pay, and does that need to connect?
- [ ] Which tools are they able to move to (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace,
      something else)?

- [ ] What is the website built on, and what part of it needs automating?

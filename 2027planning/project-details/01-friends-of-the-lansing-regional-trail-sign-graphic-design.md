# 1. Friends of the Lansing Regional Trail: sign graphic design

Project-specific gaps. Also ask the common questions in
[interview-questions.md](../interview-questions.md). Project numbers match
[project-classification.md](../project-classification.md).

- [ ] What kinds of signs (sponsor, wayfinding, rules, interpretive)?
- [ ] Exact dimensions, material, and how they are printed?
- [ ] What must appear on every sign (logos, sponsor name, text)?
- [ ] Are there brand rules or existing signs to match?
- [ ] One design, or a family of related designs?
- [ ] What file format does the printer need?
- [ ] Which sign type they are designing and for which location.
- [ ] Readability check: can it be read from walking or biking distance?

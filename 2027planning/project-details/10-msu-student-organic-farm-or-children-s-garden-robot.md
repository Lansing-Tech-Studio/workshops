# 10. MSU Student Organic Farm or Children's Garden: robot

Project-specific gaps. Also ask the common questions in
[interview-questions.md](../interview-questions.md). Project numbers match
[project-classification.md](../project-classification.md).

- [ ] Which site, and who is the contact?
- [ ] What should the robot do (monitor, water, carry, teach, entertain)?
- [ ] Indoors or outdoors? Does it need to survive weather?
- [ ] Is there a budget for parts?
- [ ] Can students visit the site, and how often?
- [ ] What safety rules apply around visitors, children, and crops?
- [ ] Is a tabletop prototype or design study useful if a working robot is
      out of reach?

Student fills in:

- [ ] One-sentence task for the robot.
- [ ] Parts list and cost.
